Ethical Issues in Bionic Integration

The advancement of bionic technology raises major ethical questions that society must address as these technologies become more accessible around the world.

Human Identity and Enhancement

Where do we draw the line between human and machine? As bionic limbs and neural interfaces overcome biological boundaries we must ask, does enhancing physical or cognitive abilities risk eroding what makes us fundamentally human? This isn't just sci-fi as it's happening now with prosthetics that restore natural movement (Xie et al., 2024). Should there be limits to augmentation or do we embrace becoming "more than human"?

Access and Inequality

Will bionics become a luxury for the wealthy? Current costs put advanced prosthetics out of reach for many which in turn is risking a future where only the privileged can enhance their abilities. With healthcare systems across the world this begins to clash with our values of fairness. Should countries cover neural implants and what happens when enhanced workers outperform others?

Privacy and Data Security

Who owns your thoughts if they're stored in a chip? Military-grade exoskeletons and consumer neural tech collect brain data vulnerable to hacking. Imagine your prosthetic hand being remotely disabled or worse your emotions being curated for ads. This means privacy laws must evolve to protect "neuro-rights" however can we be completely sure this is upheld?
